Common questions

Prayer times look a few minutes off. Open Settings and check your city, calculation method and Asr method (Hanafi or Shafiʿi). You can also adjust each prayer by minutes to match your local masjid.

Reminders stopped. iOS allows an app to schedule a limited number of reminders ahead. Open Istiqamah at least once every few days and it schedules the next ones.

I changed phones. Restore your iPhone backup, or use Settings → Backup to save a file and restore it on the new phone.

Restore purchases. Settings → Istiqamah Plus → Restore.
