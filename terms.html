Subscriptions

Istiqamah Plus is offered as auto-renewing subscriptions and a Ramadan pass. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews automatically unless it is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Everything you recorded stays readable after a plan ends.

Religious content

The app gives sources for its verses, hadith and rulings. It is a personal tracker and does not replace a qualified scholar.
