Privacy policy

Istiqamah Tracker · last updated 28 October 2026

Istiqamah is a personal worship tracker. What you record stays on your iPhone. A few optional features talk to our small server; each one is described below, with exactly what it sends. None of them sends your prayers, fasts, journal, debts, zakat, period dates, recordings, location or name.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you record is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iCloud or computer backup, which Apple encrypts and controls. If you turn on iCloud sync, Apple's iCloud key-value storage keeps a copy in your own iCloud account; we cannot read it. You can also make your own backup file from Settings.

Features that use our server (all optional)

Our server runs on Cloudflare. Like any web service, Cloudflare sees the network address of a request to deliver it; we do not store it. Nothing below is linked to an account, an email (except school and masjid accounts), an advertising id or your Apple ID.

  • Tanāfus (only if you join): once a day, this month's totals (for example how many prayers on time and pages read), your city and country, and a random number that changes every month. Optionally, the team code of a school or masjid you joined. Results are shown only as averages for groups of at least 50 people per city, 30 per country and 10 per team.
  • Group khatm and accountability partner (only if you create or join one): the nickname you type and the juz or weekly progress you share with that group.
  • Tahajjud wake circle (only if you join): your iPhone's push-notification token, time zone, language, the hours you are willing to wake others, and, only if you choose to, whether you are a brother or sister so that only the same gender is matched.
  • Dua for a stranger (only if you ask): the need you pick from a fixed list, brother or sister, your country and a random id. Others see only “Abdullah 482 asks for dua for …”.
  • School and masjid postcards (school and masjid accounts only, for teachers and imams, never children): the school or masjid name, city, official email, website and the contact person you give, checked by hand. A postal address is optional and shown only to other verified schools and masjids. Postcards carry ready phrases, a class label and a short teacher note; links, emails and phone numbers are blocked.
  • Content updates: the app downloads notices, helpline updates and text corrections. It sends nothing about you.
  • Anonymous usage counts (off unless you turn it on): once a day, how many times each screen was opened, the app version and language. No id of any kind.
  • Error reports (off unless you turn it on): when something breaks, the error message, where in the app it happened, the app version, language and screen. Phone file paths and long numbers are removed.

Data from these features is deleted when it is no longer needed: Tanāfus reports after about 13 months, usage counts after about 13 months, error reports after about 4 months, dua requests about a month after they close, and wake-circle data as soon as you leave.

Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on the iPhone itself. The wake circle uses push notifications only if you join it.
  • Location (optional): used when you tap “Use my location” to work out prayer times and the Qibla, and, only if you turn on “Notice when I arrive”, to notice when you reach the masjids you saved. It stays on your iPhone.
  • Compass: read only while the Qibla screen is open.
  • Microphone (optional): only while you record a sermon or lesson. Recordings stay on your iPhone.
  • Face ID (optional): handled by iOS to unlock your journal; the app never sees your face data.
  • Photos (optional): only to save a certificate, card or report image when you ask.
  • Calendar (optional): only when you tap “Add to iPhone Calendar”.
  • Apple Health (optional): writes Mindful Minutes for dhikr if you turn it on, and reads steps only around arriving at your masjid. Health data never leaves your iPhone.
  • Alarms (optional): the Fajr and Tahajjud alarms you choose.

Translation

When you translate a question for your scholar, Apple's on-device translation is used. When you send the question, it goes through the app you choose (WhatsApp, Messages or Mail) directly to the person you choose.

Purchases

Subscriptions, the Ramadan pass, tips and offer codes are handled by Apple. We receive no payment or personal details.

Children

Kids mode and family profiles are stored only on your iPhone. Children cannot use the server features above, and school or masjid postcards are sent by verified teachers and imams, never by children.

Your choices

Each server feature can be left at any time from inside the app, and its data is then deleted or expires as described above. To ask about or delete anything, write to us.
