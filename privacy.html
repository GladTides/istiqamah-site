Privacy policy

Istiqamah Tracker · last updated 24 October 2026

Istiqamah is a personal worship tracker. It was built so that nothing you record leaves your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We cannot see your prayers, fasts, journal, debts, zakat, period dates, recordings or anything else you enter.

Where your data lives

Everything is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iCloud or computer backup of the iPhone, which Apple encrypts and controls. You can also make your own backup file from Settings and keep it wherever you choose.

Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on the iPhone itself.
  • Location (optional): used once, only when you tap “Use my location”, to work out prayer times and the Qibla. It is not stored beyond the coordinates of your chosen place and is never sent anywhere.
  • Compass: read only while the Qibla screen is open.
  • Microphone (optional): used only while you record a sermon or lesson. Recordings stay on your iPhone.
  • Face ID (optional): handled by iOS to unlock your journal; the app never sees your face data.
  • Photos (optional): only when you choose to save a certificate or report image.

Translation

When you translate a question for your scholar, Apple's on-device translation is used. When you send the question, it goes through the app you choose (WhatsApp, Messages or Mail) directly to the person you choose.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the Ramadan pass are handled by Apple. We receive no payment or personal details.

Children

Family profiles are stored only on your iPhone. We do not collect information from anyone.
