For schools and masjids

Istiqamah Tracker is free for verified schools, madrasas and masjids.

Free for your school or masjid. Once we have verified your school or masjid, we email a free App Store code for Istiqamah Plus with your verification code. There is nothing to pay and no contract.

Postcards between schools and masjids

Classes and masjid groups exchange postcards with partners in other cities and countries: a design, up to three ready phrases (salam, a dua, “we read Juz ʿAmma this month”…) and a short teacher note, signed with a class label.

  • Digital, printable or real post. Every card can be shown on a screen or printed on A6 card. Sending real post is optional; schools that want to receive real cards can add a postal address.
  • Addresses stay private. Only other verified schools and masjids can see an address. Everyone else sees only how many cards were exchanged.
  • Safe for children. Children never type, sign their names or share photos. Notes cannot contain links, emails or phone numbers, and any card can be reported.
  • Pairing. Share your invite code with a school you know, or accept a suggested partner from another country.

Tanāfus teams

Your school or masjid gets a team code. Students, parents or your community enter it in Tanāfus to compete in good deeds together. Only team totals and averages are shown, and only once at least 10 people have joined; no one can see who is in the team or what any member did.

How to apply

  1. Install Istiqamah on the iPhone of a teacher, imam or staff member.
  2. Open Settings → School and masjid postcards → Register our school or masjid.
  3. Enter the name, city, country and the official email of the school or masjid (not a personal address), and your name and role.
  4. We check every application by hand, usually within a few days, and email an 8-character code and your free App Store code to the official address.
  5. Back in the app, tap I have a code, enter the email and code, and you're verified.
